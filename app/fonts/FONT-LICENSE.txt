Fonts shipped with this app
=============================

LiberationSans-Bold.ttf and LiberationSans-Regular.ttf are the
"Liberation Sans" typeface, Copyright (c) 2012 Red Hat, Inc.

Liberation Sans is licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1 (26 February 2007). The full license text is at
https://openfontlicense.org/ and in the upstream package
(fonts-liberation). The font files are used unmodified.

Summary of the OFL 1.1 terms: the font may be freely used,
studied, modified and redistributed, including bundled with
software, provided the font is not sold on its own and any
modified version is distributed under the same license with a
different name. This app bundles the unmodified files.
