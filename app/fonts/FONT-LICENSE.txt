Fonts shipped with this app
=============================

LiberationSans-Bold.ttf and LiberationSans-Regular.ttf are the Liberation
Sans fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://scripts.sil.org/OFL). The OFL allows the fonts to be used, studied,
copied, modified and redistributed, including bundled with this app, provided
the fonts are not sold on their own and the license text travels with them.

The Liberation fonts were designed by Steve Matteson / Ascender Corp. and
are distributed with most Linux systems (here: /usr/share/fonts/truetype/
liberation/). No Rockstar Games font, logo or artwork is used anywhere in
this app.
