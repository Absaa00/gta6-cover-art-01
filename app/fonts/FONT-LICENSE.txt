Fonts shipped with Leonida Nights Cover Maker
================================================

LiberationSans-Bold.ttf and LiberationSans-Regular.ttf are the
Liberation Sans fonts, released under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1 (26 February 2007). The full license text is available
at https://openfontlicense.org/ and in the Liberation fonts
package shipped by most Linux distributions.

The fonts are used here only as text rendering assets inside this
app. No Rockstar Games font, logo or artwork is used anywhere in
this project.
